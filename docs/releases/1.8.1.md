**Mandrake 1.8.1: nightly, the community on Discord.** Settings > About now shows where Mandrake's community is: nightly, the Discord of Mandrake and the other Nightshade apps, with a QR code for your phone.

## What changed

- **Join the community.** Settings > About > **Join the community** shows the server's name, nightly, and its link, `discord.gg/pSqgRU3YP9`. Its card has a QR code that opens the invitation on your phone.
- **Once, after this update.** The first start's question (the factory settings, or yours) has one line about nightly under its answers. Either answer puts it away for good: later updates ask their question without it.
- **In all five languages**, with the name and the link as they are.

Everything else is 1.8.0. Built and checked by the host tests (the QR code made by the app's own encoder and read back: it opens exactly `https://discord.gg/pSqgRU3YP9`); not yet run on a console.

## Install

- `PPSA98510.zip`: **Check for updates** in Settings > About installs it, or close Mandrake, extract the ZIP and copy its `PPSA98510` folder over your current one (usually `/data/homebrew/PPSA98510`). As after every update, the first start asks whether to start from the factory settings: **○** keeps yours.
- `SwanStationPS5-DiscDaemon.elf`: the disc daemon, sent once to the console's ELF loader (port 9021), as before.
- `SHA256SUMS`: the checksums of both. `gh attestation verify PPSA98510.zip -R Oliv3rmoon/Mandrake` checks that this repository's workflow built it.
